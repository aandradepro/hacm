# Slide 1

## Enterprise analytics rarely fail because of technology.

# They fail because organizations lose control over meaning.

### Body

As organizations grow, analytical capabilities evolve independently.

Business units create different KPIs.

Projects implement different business rules.

Platforms evolve at different speeds.

Governance becomes fragmented.

Technology continues to scale.

Business trust does not.

### Executive Quote

> Architecture exists to coordinate meaning before systems coordinate data.

### SAP Perspective

The same challenge appears during SAP modernization initiatives involving BW, Datasphere and Business Data Cloud, but it is fundamentally an enterprise architecture problem rather than a product problem.

# Slide 2

## Architectural coordination comes before platform selection.

### Body

Every analytical architecture should answer four questions before selecting technology.

Who owns business meaning?

Which analytical assets should become reusable?

Which decisions should remain centralized?

Which trade-offs are intentional?

Technology should implement these answers.

Not define them.

### Architectural Trade-offs

Consistency ↔ Local Autonomy

Governance ↔ Agility

Performance ↔ Maintainability

Standardization ↔ Innovation

### Executive Quote

> Good architecture makes organizational decisions explicit before technical decisions become permanent.

### SAP Perspective

Capabilities such as Business Data Cloud, Datasphere and Data Products deliver greater value when architectural decisions already exist.

# Slide 3

## Architecture proves itself through organizational permanence.

### Case 1

Global Executive Reporting Platform

Problem

Fragmented executive reporting across multiple countries.

Architectural Decision

Shared semantic foundation.

Outcome

One enterprise reporting platform adopted globally for four consecutive years.

--------------------------------------------------

### Case 2

Enterprise KPI Calculation Engine

Problem

Duplicated KPI definitions throughout the organization.

Architectural Decision

Centralized semantic calculation layer.

Outcome

Approximately 1,800 governed corporate indicators managed through a common architecture.

--------------------------------------------------

### Case 3

Enterprise Legacy Preservation

Problem

SAP systems needed to be retired without losing historical business knowledge.

Architectural Decision

Architecture-first preservation strategy.

Outcome

Secure decommissioning with compliance and historical continuity preserved.

--------------------------------------------------

### Executive Quote

> Sustainable architecture survives organizational change.

### SAP Perspective

Although implemented in SAP environments, these architectural decisions are platform-independent and applicable across enterprise analytics ecosystems.
